The business problem is:
"Orders are increasing, but profit doesn't feel like it. Customers don't seem to come back. Where should I put next year's marketing money?"
You need to write two user stories. Each story must include:
1. A user story describing what the business needs.
2. Three acceptance criteria explaining what must be achieved.
3. A definition of done explaining when the work is considered complete.
4. The Step 5 analytical question it addresses.
5. What clarification or information you need from the BA or Product Owner before starting the sprint.
Below is an assignment-ready example.
User Story 1: Analyse product profitability
User Story 1
User story
As a Sweet_Layers business owner, I want to understand the gross profit generated by each product category so that I can identify the most profitable products and make better decisions about where to invest marketing money.
Acceptance criteria
1. The analysis must show sales revenue, product costs and gross profit for each product category, broken down by month.
2. The gross profit margin must be calculated consistently using an agreed formula:
\[
\text{Gross Margin %}=\frac{\text{Revenue - Cost}}{\text{Revenue}}\times100
\]
3. The results must identify the product categories generating the highest and lowest gross profit.
Definition of done
* The gross profit and gross margin definitions are documented and agreed with the BA or Product Owner.
* The calculated figures reconcile with the original sales and product cost data.
* The analysis can be reproduced using the documented steps and code or queries saved in the GitHub repository.
* The findings are presented in a clear table or visualisation with business recommendations.
Which Step 5 question does this cover?
The question about which products or product categories generate the highest gross profit and whether increasing sales is actually improving profitability.
What I need from the BA or Product Owner before the sprint starts:
* Confirmation of the analysis period (for example, January 2025 to June 2026).
* Sales data containing order dates, product names, quantities and selling prices.
* Product cost information so that gross profit can be calculated.
* Agreement on how discounts, refunds and cancelled orders should be treated.
* Confirmation of which product categories are most important to the business.
User Story 2: Analyse customer repeat purchases
User Story 2
User story
As a Sweet_Layers marketing manager, I want to understand how frequently customers return to make additional purchases so that I can develop marketing strategies that improve customer retention and encourage repeat purchases.
Acceptance criteria
1. The analysis must identify the number of unique customers who made purchases during the selected analysis period.
2. The analysis must calculate the repeat purchase rate, showing the percentage of customers who made more than one purchase.
3. The results must identify customer purchasing patterns and highlight opportunities to improve customer retention.
Definition of done
* The definition of a repeat customer and the repeat purchase rate are documented and agreed with the BA or Product Owner.
* Customer counts and order numbers reconcile with the original customer and sales data.
* The analysis is reproducible using documented code or queries saved in the GitHub repository.
* The findings are presented clearly, with recommendations for improving customer retention.
Which Step 5 question does this cover?
The question about whether customers are returning to make additional purchases and how customer retention has changed over time.
What I need from the BA or Product Owner before the sprint starts:
* Customer data containing unique customer IDs.
* Sales data containing customer IDs, order IDs and purchase dates.
* Confirmation of what the business considers a repeat customer.
* Agreement on the analysis period.
* Information about previous customer retention campaigns, if available.
* Confirmation of whether the analysis should compare different customer groups or purchasing periods.

What happens before and during the sprint?
1. Sprint planning
The team discusses the two user stories, confirms requirements, agrees on acceptance criteria and selects the work for the sprint.
2. Data preparation
The Data Analyst obtains the sales, customer and product data and checks data quality.
3. Analysis
The analyst calculates gross profit, gross margin and repeat purchase rates.
4. Review
The team presents the findings to the BA and Product Owner to confirm that the requirements have been met.
5. Sprint retrospective
The team discusses what went well, what could be improved and what should happen in the next sprint.
5. Summary for your assignment
The two user stories support Sweet_Layers in answering two important business questions:
1. Profitability: Which products generate the most profit, and where should the business focus its investment?
2. Customer retention: Are customers returning to make additional purchases, and how can marketing encourage them to return?
